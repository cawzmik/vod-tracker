GD Rewind

Download GD Rewind

The Geode mod that saves every attempt and links it to your Twitch VOD, so your runs show up on gdrewind.live.

GD Rewind
Download
  1. Have Geode installedGD Rewind is a Geode mod. If you don't have Geode yet, get it from geode-sdk.org.
  2. Put the file in your Geode mods folderOn Steam it's usually here:
    C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Geometry Dash\geode\mods
    Paste it into the File Explorer address bar. If GD is somewhere else, right click Geometry Dash in Steam, then Manage, Browse local files, and open geode, then mods.
  3. Restart GDClose Geometry Dash fully and open it again.
  4. Set up your TwitchOpen Geode, find GD Rewind, open its settings and type your Twitch username. That's all it needs.

You only do this once. After that the mod updates itself: when there's a new version, it downloads when you open GD and you'll see a message to restart.
